# SnipMac

SnipMac is a macOS tool for creating screenshots and screen recordings with its own focused capture experience.

## Language

**Capture**:
A screenshot or screen recording created with SnipMac.
_Avoid_: Snip, asset

**Screenshot**:
A static image of visible screen content.
_Avoid_: Still capture, screen grab

**Screen Recording**:
A video of visible screen activity.
_Avoid_: Video capture, screen record (noun)

**Capture Scope**:
The visible content included in a capture: a display, a region, or a window.
_Avoid_: Capture type, target

**Display Capture**:
A capture whose scope is one entire connected display selected by the user.
_Avoid_: Full-screen capture, main-screen capture, whole-screen capture

**Region Capture**:
A capture whose scope is a user-selected rectangular portion of the visible desktop.
_Avoid_: Area capture, selection capture

**Window Capture**:
A screenshot whose scope is one application window.
_Avoid_: App capture

**Quick-Access Preview**:
A temporary surface for reviewing, annotating, dragging, sharing, or dismissing a new capture.
_Avoid_: Editor, popup, thumbnail

**Annotation**:
A visual mark added to a screenshot, limited initially to arrows, rectangles, text, redaction, and cropping.
_Avoid_: Edit, markup
